- Refined the eight rune heads to open dense patches and use a consistent single gold stroke with one cream junction, rather than height-based color changes. Small central marks are inset within their cells to leave more clearance from the surrounding circles.
- Reduced outer rim divisions from 48 to a regular 32, aligned with the 16 principal rune positions. Removed oversized circular node clusters from the octagram, kept compact junctions, reduced the bright medallion inset and changed outer rim glints to gold. Main ring radii and design remain unchanged.
