# Crimson circle — oriented glyphs and smaller medallions

- Scope/mode: preview Implementation. User requested circumferential glyph orientation, smaller medallion borders with larger centered symbols, and removal of lines between the outer runes. This revision supersedes the earlier upright-glyph direction.
- [Combined 128×128 PNG](combined-128.png), [4× view](combined-128-4x.png); [inner Aseprite](inner.aseprite) / [PNG](inner-preview.png), [outer Aseprite](outer.aseprite) / [PNG](outer-preview.png), [glyph atlas Aseprite](glyph-atlas.aseprite) / [PNG](glyph-atlas.png). [Native authoring script](author-native.lua) reproduces these sources and split textures.
- Glyph tops now face radially outward: top 0°, right 90°, bottom 180°, left 270°. Intermediate positions follow their circumference angle, and glyph orientation advances with the corresponding rotating group. Inner and outer groups still rotate in opposite directions.
- The four small medallion frames shrink from 10×10 to 9×9 and use a single continuous contour instead of a double border. Their symbols grow from 3×3 to 5×5. Both use the same integer pixel center; odd dimensions avoid a half-pixel mismatch.
- Removed the long lines between outer runes and the short dividers at the rune band's inner edge. The outer rim ticks remain border decoration. Main circle radii, overlapping-square octagram, central circle, sixteen outer rune positions and four-color Crimson palette remain unchanged.
- Split presentation textures: [outer rings](outer-rings.png), [inner rings](inner-rings.png), [octagram](inner-star.png), [glyph atlas](glyph-atlas.png), [medallion frame](medallion.png). Both full components use 128×128 canvases; visible diameters remain 86/128px. The first half of charging scales the whole inner group; the second half scales the outer group.
- [Full charge 3×](charge-100-3x.png), [rotating 3×](charge-rotated-3x.png), [lighter floor](charge-light.png) visually inspected. These are preview compositions, not Unity screenshots.
