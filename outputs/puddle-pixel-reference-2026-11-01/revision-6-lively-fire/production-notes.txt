Artwork changes:
- Beer liquid opacity is approximately 60% (main fill alpha154/255). Whole
  visible-image mean alpha drops from231.76 to153.19. Original gold RGB palette

  from53 pixels to4; foam coverage drops from285-291 to53-57 pixels per frame.
- Fire uses broad orange/yellow flame sheets with torn branching tips, sweeping
  in different directions across the plane. Rounded boiling patches and dark
  crater-like pools are removed. Yellow/pale pixels increase from9-13 to181-192
  per frame. Ground still has no tall standing flame silhouettes baked in.
- Independent particles are smaller glowing embers. The preview has12 staggered
  emission points instead of4, with11 active instances at each time step. Each
  travels along a lateral S-curve while rising. Half the paths are mirrored.
  The baked flight changes horizontal direction twice and rises21 pixels by
  visible centroid, then disappears. Its loop period is960ms.
- Beer-to-fire transition is regenerated from the revised art. Original ground
  occupancy, center pivot, frame counts and timings remain unchanged.
