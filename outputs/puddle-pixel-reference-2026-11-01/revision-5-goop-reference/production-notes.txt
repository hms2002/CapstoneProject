The reference suggests a thin liquid surface, visible floor beneath it, and
scattered combustion accents. These are original sample pixels; reference
images were not copied into the assets. Reference animation was not observed.
Timings, ignition spread and particle motion below are proposals for this game.

Changes from revision 4:
- Beer retains bright gold and cream foam, with subtle translucency in liquid.
- Removed thick yellow combustion ribbons. Fire uses a thin red-brown liquid
  substrate, seven small flat combustion patches and intermittent hot pixels.
- Upright flames remain a separate particle sprite. The preview staggers four
  emitters, with proposed upward motion and drift; it is not a ParticleSystem.
- All ground frames retain the same nonzero footprint and pivot. Alpha values
  intentionally changed; neither binary nor unchanged alpha is claimed.

  BeerIdle 1-4, Ignite 5-12, FireLoop 13-18, ground pivot (32,32).
The final ignition frame equals the first standalone fire frame. The combined
FireLoop starts with standalone fire frame 2 to avoid a duplicate-frame hold.
Ignition currently spreads from the left; contact-dependent direction remains
an integration decision.

preserve partial alpha. The particle GIF retains binary transparency.

  960x320, exact 4x pixel scale, 3840 ms demonstration cycle.

The review floor and compositions are not Unity captures.

Layer editing:
Source layers have disjoint pixel coverage to preserve the intended RGBA
palette without repeated partial-alpha blending. Hiding an upper layer can
expose a hole, rather than an underpainted lower layer.
